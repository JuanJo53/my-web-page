# Mission: My Online CV & Tech Hub

## Vision
To create a professional and engaging personal brand platform that serves as both a high-impact digital CV for recruiters and a future educational hub for the tech community.

## Primary Objectives
1.  **Professional Showcase:** Present work experience, education, and technical skills in a clean, accessible format.
2.  **Portfolio Hub:** Display key projects with detailed descriptions, links, and visual assets.
3.  **Communication Bridge:** Facilitate direct contact through an integrated contact form.
4.  **Future Scalability:** Build a foundation that supports future expansion into technical blogging and online courses.

## Target Audience
-   **Recruiters & Tech Companies:** Looking for a quick, comprehensive overview of professional capabilities.
-   **Tech Enthusiasts:** Seeking knowledge sharing through future blog posts and course content.

## Tone & Aesthetic
-   **Professional yet Tech-Forward:** Maintaining a balance between corporate readiness and a modern, innovative feel.
-   **Consistent Branding:** Utilizing the established turquoise and white color palette defined in the project's assets.
